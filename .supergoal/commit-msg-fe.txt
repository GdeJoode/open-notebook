feat(frontend): chunk merge/split toolbar in inspect workspace (I.D-3)

Add mergeChunk/splitChunk API methods, useMergeChunk/useSplitChunk
TanStack Query hooks, and a ChunkActionsToolbar rendered in the
active-chunk section of the inspect properties pane. Merge folds the
active chunk into the next chunk (disabled when last); split breaks it
at a typed character offset (1..len-1). Both show pending state,
aria-labels, and toast on failure, and invalidate the chunks query on
success.
