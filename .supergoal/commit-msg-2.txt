feat(frontend): add mode + controlled selection to PdfChunkViewer (I.B)

Add an optional `mode: "embed" | "fullscreen"` prop (default "embed", so the
Chunks tab is unchanged) and optional controlled-selection props
(`selectedChunkId` / `onSelectChunkId`). In fullscreen mode the internal
chunk-list pane is suppressed and the border dropped, since the inspect
workspace supplies its own chunk list and paneling. Controlled selection
lets the workspace's ChunkListPanel drive the overlay highlight and page
follow without the viewer owning selection state.
